We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, payment information, or advertising ID. The app contains no ads and no in-app purchases. A custom avatar picture you choose stays on your device and is not uploaded.

2. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage) operated by Google LLC to store account and game data. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms. We do not sell or share your personal data with any other third party.

3. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your saved data is restricted to your own account by server-side security rules.

4. What other players can see

Your player name, level, power, hero, arena points, guild membership and guild chat messages are visible to other players.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Privacy & Account → Hapus Akun (Delete Account).
  • On the web: follow the steps at cardbattlerpg.online/delete-account.

Deletion permanently removes your login, profile, cloud save, ranking entry, guild membership and your chat messages within 30 days. Reports you submitted about other players may be kept for up to 12 months to prevent abuse, without being linked to your deleted account’s profile.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us. We respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app.
