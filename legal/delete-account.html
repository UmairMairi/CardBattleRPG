Delete your Card Battle RPG account

App: Card Battle RPG (com.pegasus.cardbattlerpg) · Developer: [DEVELOPER NAME]

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open Card Battle RPG and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to Privacy & Account and tap Hapus Akun (Delete Account).
  3. Email accounts: enter your password. Type HAPUS to confirm and tap Hapus Permanen.

Option 2 — Without the app

Email support@cardbattlerpg.online from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. Guest players: include your player name and, if possible, your user ID (Profile screen). We may ask you to verify ownership. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email/guest account) and profile
  • Cloud save: level, gold, diamonds, cards, heroes, inventory, story progress
  • Ranking entry and online status
  • Guild membership and all guild chat messages you sent; guilds you lead are deleted if no members remain
  • Online battle rooms and matchmaking entries
  • Your blocked-players list
  • The local save on the device where you delete the account

What may be kept

Moderation reports you filed about others (up to 12 months, for abuse prevention). Deletion cannot be undone.
